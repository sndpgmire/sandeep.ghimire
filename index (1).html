Who I Am

About Me

I am a strategic and purpose-driven Public Health professional with over ten years of experience in Nepal's development sector. My work centres on Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) systems, and I have led multi-sector programs across organisations including FAIRMED Foundation, CARE Nepal, and International Nepal Fellowship.

I am deeply committed to equity and the "leave no one behind" principle, leveraging technology and data visualisation tools to enhance program impact. I have designed and operationalised MEAL frameworks, KPIs, logical models, and digital data collection systems that drive evidence-based decision making.

Beyond implementation, I am passionate about mentoring emerging M&E professionals, facilitating knowledge-sharing workshops, and building institutional learning cultures. I hold an MPhil in Public Administration from Tribhuvan University and an MPH in Global Health from Thammasat University, Thailand.
